BokyLearn

Fun-fact doom-scrolling learning app.

Bring your own key Android Flutter

BokyLearn turns idle scrolling into something. It shows you short facts, one card at a time. When a fact interests you, you can ask for a longer expansion or a follow-up question — answered by whichever AI provider you configured, with your own API key. Bookmark what you like and filter the feed towards what you actually want to read.

Features

  • Short-fact feed. A steady stream of compact, genuinely interesting facts rather than an infinite content treadmill.
  • Bring-your-own-key AI. Expansions and follow-ups go to the provider you chose — OpenAI, OpenRouter, Anthropic, Gemini, Ollama (cloud or local), or OpenCode Go. No Boky proxy, no shared key, no billing through us.
  • Monthly fact cache. A refresh of the fact set keeps the feed fresh without hammering any service every time you open the app.
  • Interest filters. Nudge the feed towards the subjects you care about, and bookmark the ones worth keeping.
  • Mature-content preference. A setting you control, so the feed can stay suitable for the situation you are in.
  • Wikipedia grounding. Where available, expansions are grounded in encyclopedia references rather than pure model recall.
  • Secure key storage. API keys are held in the platform's secure storage (Keystore-backed on Android), not in plain preferences.
  • No Boky analytics. Your reading habits are not our business.

Your data

API keys are stored in the platform's secure storage. Cached facts and your bookmarks live locally on the device. When you ask for an expansion, the request goes to the AI provider you selected, authenticated with your key — which means that provider's own privacy policy applies to that request, not ours. BokyApps runs no analytics service and receives no copy of it.
