Free, open-source software that keeps your data yours.

BokyApps is a small family of apps built by Sarel Myburgh. Every one of them is free and open source under a recognised licence. There are no ads, no subscriptions, no tracking SDKs and no Boky-operated cloud backend — because there is nothing behind the app that needs your data.

The Boky ethos

  • Free. No ads, no in-app purchases to unlock features, no upsells.
  • Open source. The source is public, and the code — not just the marketing — is the authoritative description of what the app does.
  • Secure by default. On-device processing wherever possible, and when a service is needed you bring your own API key.
  • Bring your own key. We do not proxy your requests through our infrastructure. Your key talks to the provider you chose.
  • No analytics. No telemetry, no crash reporters, no advertising identifiers, no fingerprinting.
  • Self-hostable. The server apps ship as Docker Compose stacks you run yourself. Your data lives on your machine.

Where the code lives

The apps and this site live under the BokyApps organisation. Sarel Myburgh maintains them (github.com/sarel-myburgh).

No store, no middleman

BokyQR and BokyLearn are distributed as builds from their own GitHub release pages (and F-Droid listings when they exist), not through a store account that we control. BokyDo and BokyDojo are self-hosted servers. Wherever a store listing has not been created yet, we say so plainly instead of linking to a page that does not exist.

Privacy, in one page

All Boky apps share a single privacy policy, published at /privacy/. That page is the canonical hosted version; the PRIVACY.md files inside individual app repositories may mirror it. The short version: nothing is collected from you, because there is no service collecting it.

This site itself sets no cookies, runs no analytics, loads no third-party fonts, scripts or images, and is served as static files.
