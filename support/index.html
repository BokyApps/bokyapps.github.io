Support the creators

Boky apps stay free and ad-free. That is the whole point, and it is not going to change. Optional support helps cover the real costs: hosting, build servers, app-signing infrastructure, domains and the hours after work.

There is nothing to unlock. Donating does not add a feature, remove a limitation, or buy you priority support. Every feature in every app is available to everyone for the same price, which is zero. If you can spare something, it helps; if you cannot, the apps are yours to use regardless.

Other ways to help

Money is not the only useful contribution, and for most people it is not the most valuable one:

  • Report bugs properly. Reproducible steps, app version, device and logs beat "it crashes".
  • Improve the documentation. Getting a self-hosted stack running is the hardest part of shipping a server app.
  • Help other users. Answer questions in issues and discussions, so the maintainer's inbox stays useful.
  • Tell people. A share or a mention costs nothing and helps more than you would think.
  • Contribute code. All four apps are open source.

What you will always get for free

  • Every feature, in every app, at no cost.
  • The source code, under an open-source licence.
  • No ads, and none planned.
  • No analytics or telemetry, ever.
  • No account, no sign-in, and no Boky-operated cloud backend to opt out of.
